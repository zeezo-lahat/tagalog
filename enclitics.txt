                                         syllables
------------------- 1 -------------------
na/pa                                        1
------------------- 2 -------------------
man - even (if, though), although (as kahit) 1
------- 3a never follow class 4 ---------
nga - indeed, affirmation                    1
ba                                           1
din/rin                                      1
lang/lamang                                  1
kasí                                         2
po/ho                                        1
-------- 3b may follow class 4 -----------
daw/raw                                      1
muna - for now, first, beforehand            2
naman - contrast, emphasis; also             2
------------------- 4 -------------------
kayâ - therefore; I wonder, perhaps          2
palá - realization, apparently               2
sana - hope, unrealized condition            2
tulóy - cause and effect, as a result        2
yatà - it seems, apparently                  2

3a and 3b can intermingle, however, this is the most commonly used ordering:
nga rin/din lamang/lang daw/raw po/ho ba muna naman kasi

two-syllable personal pronouns follow the above:
niya namin natin ninyo nila  ako siya kami tayo kayo sila

Note: all the two-syllable 'ng' personal pronouns start with 'n'


fyi: sa personal pronouns: (These are not enclytics)
      1st person start with 'a': akin, amin, atin
      2nd person start with 'i': iyo, inyo
      3rd person start with 'k': kaniya, kanila
